🕹 How to Play


    

      
Flippers

      

        
← Z A ⇧ L
Left flipper

        
→ . / D ⇧ R
Right flipper

      

    


    

      
Ball & Game

      

        
Space / Enter
Hold to pull back plunger, release to launch ball

        
N
Nudge the table — too many nudges = TILT!

      

    


    

      
View & Camera

      

        
Drag
Rotate the camera around the table

        
Scroll wheel / Pinch
Zoom in / out

        
Double-click
Reset camera to default view

        
R
Reset camera view

      

    


    

      
Options

      

        
T
Cycle through themes

        
M
Toggle background music on / off

        
B
Toggle bloom / glow effect

        
H / ?
Show / hide this help screen

        
Esc
Close menus & this screen

      

    


    

      
Scoring

      

        
Bumper hit
100 pts — every 10th hit: +1 000 bonus!

        
Slingshot
10 pts

        
Post hit
25 pts

      

    


    

      📱 Mobile: Use the ◀ ▶ buttons to control flippers, the centre button to launch, and the TILT button to nudge. Shake your device to nudge too!
